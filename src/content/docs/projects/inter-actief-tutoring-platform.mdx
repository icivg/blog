## About the project

I co-developed a full-stack tutoring platform for the Inter-Actief study association. Students can find a tutor that fits them, and tutors can offer their help. Our team won the **Best Project award of the Data & Information module (M4) 2024–2025**, presented by Inter-Actief on 2 July 2025, and the association asked to use the platform for real.

## Features

- **Find a tutor:** search by tutor name or subject and filter by study, availability, location, gender and price range. You can also choose to see only tutors who were a teaching assistant for that subject.
- **Become a tutor:** tutors create a profile with the subjects they teach, the courses they were a TA for, and where they can meet.
- **Admin approval:** new tutor accounts stay pending until an administrator approves them.
