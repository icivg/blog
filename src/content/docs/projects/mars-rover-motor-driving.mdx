## About the project

At [RoboTeam Twente](/about/#what-im-involved-in), we designed and built a Mars rover to compete in the **European Rover Challenge (ERC)**. I developed and led the work on the rover's embedded motor control system. It runs FreeRTOS on STM32 microcontrollers and talks to the motor drivers over PWM.

## What I worked on

- Developing the motor control firmware with FreeRTOS on STM32 microcontrollers
- Implementing PWM-based communication with the motor drivers
- Leading the embedded motor control work

## Challenges

Getting the timing right in real time, keeping the motor control precise, and designing the system as a whole.
