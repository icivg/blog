## About the project

Beginner basketball players often don't know what's wrong with their shooting form, and they rarely have a coach watching. Our user research confirmed that need, so we built a web app that watches your shot through the camera and tells you what to fix.

You record a shot, and the app tracks your body with a pose-detection skeleton. It then checks your shooting elbow, knee bend, shoulder alignment, release extension and posture against target ranges, and explains what went well and what to improve.

## What I worked on

- **Design and implementation:** I helped design and implement the hi-fi prototype as a web application using **Vue.js** and **MediaPipe Pose**. That included camera recording, voice guidance, and visual feedback features.
- **User testing:** I took part in testing the prototype with users, observing how they interacted with camera positioning, feedback, history, and filtering features.
- **Improvements:** based on the test results, I helped identify usability issues and suggest improvements such as hands-free recording, clearer favorites functionality, and more consistent feedback.
