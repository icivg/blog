## About the project

I developed and led the work on the embedded motor control systems for a Mars rover. The system runs FreeRTOS on STM32 microcontrollers and talks to the motor drivers over PWM.

## What I worked on

- Developing the motor control firmware with FreeRTOS on STM32 microcontrollers
- Implementing PWM-based communication with the motor drivers
- Leading the embedded motor control work

## Challenges

Getting the timing right in real time, keeping the motor control precise, and designing the system as a whole.
