Hi! I'm Irem, a software developer and student in Enschede, Netherlands. I love building full-stack applications, with Java and PostgreSQL on the backend and Vue.js on the frontend, and I enjoy the system design that ties them together: how components, data and services fit, and how to keep them maintainable as they grow. I care about making software that's genuinely useful, so I like to start from user research and testing. My [award-winning tutoring platform](/projects/inter-actief-tutoring-platform/) is one example.

I've also worked close to the hardware, writing embedded C with FreeRTOS on STM32 microcontrollers, so I'm comfortable anywhere from the interface down to the metal. Outside my studies I spend most of my time in student teams and communities, bringing people together and making things happen.
